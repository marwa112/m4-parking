Red labels mark cleaning that bans parking at the start time or within 7 days after it, with the date of the next ban; dashed labels are cleaning rules that don't apply that soon, for example because of odd/even weeks or the season.

Your M4 permit is valid on signs marked M with taxa 4 or higher: M4, M4n, M5 and M5n. "n" signs allow parking weekdays 18:00–09:00, and from 15:00 the day before a Sunday or public holiday, until 09:00 the next weekday. Same spot at most 14 days in a row.

Data: Göteborgs Stad open parking data and OpenStreetMap addresses.

Not included: temporary bans (roadworks, events). Always check the signs where you park.
