The problem

One permit. A rulebook per block.

M4

Valid only on signs with your zone letter and the same or a higher taxa: M4, M4n, M5, M5n.

M4n

"n" signs cover residents weekdays 18:00–09:00, from 15:00 before Sundays and public holidays.

⚠ Wed 09–12

Street cleaning bans everyone, on different days, odd or even weeks, and seasons per section.

14 days

At most 14 days in a row on the same spot. Then you move.
